NovaByte Systems Partners With Summit Health Care For Beta Trial

Published: July 14, 2026 | Media & Public Relations

SAN FRANCISCO — NovaByte Systems today announced the inaugural deployment partner for its high-security private cloud platform, officially codenamed Project Aegis Vault.

First Enterprise Health Pilot Confirmed

NovaByte has entered an exclusive testing agreement with Summit Health Care Group. Dr. Arthur Vance's informatics division will test the Aegis Vault protocol against strict medical privacy standards.

“Partnering with Summit Health Care Group allows us to validate our zero-knowledge architecture ahead of our planned November 14 public rollout,” stated David Vance, Chief Executive Officer.

Lead Architect Alex Mercer (alexm-novabyte) noted that testing nodes have been initialized at /test-portal/, with logistics and banking modules to follow in late Q3.
